Safety first: TipsyBuddy gives rough estimates only. It can never tell you that you are safe to drive. Never drink and drive. If someone needs urgent help, call your local emergency number immediately.

1. Who can use TipsyBuddy

TipsyBuddy is only for adults of legal drinking age where they live. By using it, you confirm you meet that requirement.

2. What TipsyBuddy does

  • Helps you log drinks, see estimates of how alcohol may be affecting you, and review your history.
  • Lets you voluntarily share your live location, venue, and status with friends via a private link.
  • Provides quick links to rideshare services (Uber, Lyft), calling, and maps to help you get home.

3. Estimates are not advice

BAC levels, sobriety times, hydration scores, and hangover forecasts are simplified estimates for harm reduction and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, or professional advice. Food, sleep, body composition, medications, and many other factors affect alcohol. Do not rely on TipsyBuddy to decide whether to drive, work, or do anything safety-sensitive.

4. Use location sharing responsibly

  • Only share your location when you mean to, only with people you trust, and only for as long as needed.
  • Only track someone else's session if they sent you the link themselves.
  • Do not use live links to stalk, harass, or monitor anyone without their clear permission.
  • Remember that anyone you send the link to can forward it to others while sharing is active.
