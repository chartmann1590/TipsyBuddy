Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

TipsyBuddy is built to help you have a fun night out and get home safely — without giving up your privacy. This policy explains in plain language what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we store on your phone

Your drinks, settings, and preferences (like weight, home address, and emergency contact) are stored on your own device. They stay there unless you choose to share something.

2. What we collect when you share live

If you turn on Live Sharing in the app, we temporarily store the following so friends you invite can see it on the live tracker page:

  • Your display name, venue/check-in name, and status message
  • Your live GPS location and when it was last updated
  • Your estimated BAC level shown in the app
  • Your phone battery level
  • Contact details you chose to include (such as phone number or home address for ride pickups)
  • Sharing expiry time, and "wave" timestamps when friends check in on you

Live sessions expire automatically (30 minutes to 4 hours, your choice). You can stop sharing anytime in the app, which stops further updates. Only people you send the link to can view your session — there is no public directory of sessions.

3. What friends' browsers share

When someone opens your live link, their browser loads the page from our hosting provider. We do not require friends to sign up or install anything. If they tap "Send Wave," we record the time of that wave so your phone can show a notification.

4. How we use information

  • To show your live location and status to people you invited, while sharing is on
  • To provide drink tracking, estimates, and safety features in the app
  • To keep the service running, prevent abuse, and fix problems

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your location for advertising.

5. Service providers

  • Hosting & live sync: Firebase Hosting and Firestore (Google) deliver the tracker page and pass live updates between your phone and your friends' browsers.
  • Maps: OpenStreetMap tiles display the live map. Your approximate location is sent to the map tile provider as part of loading map images, as with any online map.
  • Rides & calls: If you or a friend taps Uber, Lyft, or Call, you'll be sent to that service (Uber, Lyft, or your phone dialer). Their own privacy policies apply from that point.
  • Ads (in the Android app): the app may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve and measure ads. You can learn more in Google's advertising privacy info.

6. Cookies

TipsyBuddy does not set any first-party tracking cookies and does not profile or sell your data. The tracker page does, however, rely on third-party services that may set cookies of their own. Your consent choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under tipsybuddy_cookie_consent_v1 (not in a cookie), so saving a choice simply dismisses the banner.

Third-party services that may set cookies when you visit the tracker page:

  • Firebase (Hosting & Firestore) / Google — serves the page and streams live updates.
  • OpenStreetMap tiles — serves the map tiles shown on the live map.
  • Leaflet (unpkg CDN) — delivers the Leaflet CSS/JS used to render the map; only loaded if you accept optional cookies.
  • Google Fonts — provides the site fonts; only loaded if you accept optional cookies.

See our full Cookie Policy for details. Change your choice anytime with the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

7. How long we keep data

Live session data is kept only to operate active sharing and for a short period afterward to handle expired links gracefully. Data on your phone stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

8. Your choices

  • Don't turn on Live Sharing if you don't want to share your location — everything else in the app works without it.
  • Only send live links to people you trust.
  • Stop sharing anytime in the app, or let the timer expire.
  • Delete drinks, history, or profile details in the app at any time.
  • You can deny location permission — sharing just won't work without it.

9. Drinking age

TipsyBuddy is intended only for adults of legal drinking age in their location. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.

11. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/chartmann1590/TipsyBuddy.
